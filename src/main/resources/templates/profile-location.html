Set your college community

To show nearby equipment and prevent long-distance delivery, tell Equiply your college and the campus/usual pickup area you can collect items from.

Use this while at campus or your usual pickup area. You can enter coordinates manually if needed.

Same-college students can access each other's listings. Students from other colleges can access listings when their saved pickup area is within 10 km. Exact coordinates are used for the distance check and are not displayed publicly.
